
# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:
- Being respectful to others.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Helping other community members.
- Using friendly and professional language.

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment or discrimination.
- Insulting or disrespecting others.
- Sharing inappropriate content.
- Unprofessional communication.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for explaining and enforcing our standards.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public communication related to the project.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take appropriate action when someone violates these standards.
